# About this repository

This is the open-source core of Mlola UI, published under the MIT licence: the
engine and its five themes, behaviour, motion, scene, icons, the free
components and the CLI.

It is exported from the main repository, where code generation and the
quality audits run; the generated stylesheets, tokens and registry here are
their output. Issues and pull requests are welcome; accepted changes are
applied upstream and arrive here with the next export.

Mlola Pro (AI, code, canvas, editor, workflow and chart components, plus
blocks, pages and templates) is a commercial product and is not part of this
repository. See https://ui.mlola.com/pricing.
